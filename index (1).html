What’s holding your business back?

  • Sales activity is high, but conversions remain low.
  • Leads come in, but follow-up is slow or inconsistent.
  • The CRM is full of data, yet nobody trusts the pipeline.
  • Growth has stalled despite more effort.
  • Teams lack clear targets, accountability or KPIs.
  • Education enquiries are not turning into admissions.
  • Founders are still involved in every deal.
  • Expansion into India or a new market has no clear roadmap.

An audit looks for root causes, not symptoms, so your team spends effort on the few fixes that matter most.

How I can help

How an engagement works

  1. Discover

    A call to understand your business, goals, market and current sales set-up.

  2. Audit

    Review of lead sources, follow-ups, CRM workflow, funnel and conversion, using the data you have.

  3. Recommend

    Prioritised findings: what is leaking, what to fix first, and why.

  4. Act

    A practical 30-day action plan and a final review meeting. Ongoing advisory is optional.

Recommendations and advisory are the core deliverables. Implementation is yours unless a separate arrangement is agreed.
